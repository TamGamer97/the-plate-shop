Terms & Refunds

Last updated: 20 October 2026

These terms apply to every order placed on theplateshop.net. Please read them before you order, especially the section on cancelling, because number plates are made to order and personalised to you.

Who we are

This website is run by The Plate Shop. You can contact us by email at theplateshop@hotmail.com or on WhatsApp.

Your order

  • Your order is accepted when your payment goes through and you see the confirmation page. We will also send a receipt to your email.
  • Prices are shown in pounds sterling and include everything listed in your basket. Delivery and fitting charges are shown before you pay.
  • Please check your registration, plate style, size and colours carefully before paying. We make your plates exactly as entered.
  • If we cannot fulfil your order, for example because a style is unavailable or we cannot verify your documents, we will contact you and give you a full refund if we cannot sort it out.

Road-legal and show plates

  • By law, we can only make road-legal plates once we have checked your identity (such as a driving licence) and that you are entitled to the registration (such as the V5C logbook or certificate of entitlement). We will ask for these before your plates are made or handed over.
  • If you cannot provide valid documents, we will cancel your order and refund you in full.
  • Show plates, including tinted plates and any plate with non-standard spacing, fonts or styling, are not legal for use on public roads. They are sold for shows, display and off-road use only. It is your responsibility to use them lawfully.

Click & collect

  • Click & collect is free, from The Plate Shop, Oldham, OL2 5LW. Same-day collection is available for many orders. We will text you when your plates are ready, with directions.
  • Please bring your ID and proof of entitlement when collecting road-legal plates.
  • If you added fitting, we will fit your plates when you collect them. Fitting is £9.99 per plate.

Delivery

  • Delivery is free to Oldham postcodes (OL1, OL2, OL3, OL4, OL8, OL9 and M35), usually the same day or next day.
  • Delivery everywhere else in the UK is £9.99 by tracked service and usually takes 2–3 days.
  • Delivery times are estimates and start once your plates are made and any documents have been checked. We will keep you updated if there is a delay.
  • If your plates arrive damaged, please contact us within 48 hours with a photo and we will replace them free of charge.

Cancelling your order

Normally you have 14 days to cancel an online order. This right does not apply to goods made to your specification or clearly personalised, which includes number plates made with your registration.

  • Before we start making your plates, you can cancel for a full refund. Contact us as soon as possible.
  • Once we have started making your plates, the order cannot be cancelled or refunded for a change of mind.

Mistakes and faulty plates

If something is wrong with your plates, please contact us with your order details and a photo as soon as you can.

  • If we made a mistake, for example the wrong registration, style, size or colour compared with your order, we will remake your plates free of charge.
  • If your plates are faulty, for example damaged, peeling or not made to standard, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of receiving them. After that, we will repair or replace them, or give you a refund if that is not possible.
  • If the error is in the details you entered, such as a typo in your registration, we can make new plates at our normal price.

This does not affect your legal rights. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, goods must be as described, fit for purpose and of satisfactory quality. For more information, visit Citizens Advice.
